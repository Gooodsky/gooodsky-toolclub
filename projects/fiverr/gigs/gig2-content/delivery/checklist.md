# Delivery Checklist — AI Content Generation System

Use this checklist before marking any order as delivered. Check off each item as it is completed.

---

## Basic Package — $500 (Single-Platform Content Generator)

### Included
- [ ] 1 content type configured (e.g., product descriptions OR social posts OR ad copy)
- [ ] Single LLM integration (GPT-4o or Claude)
- [ ] Brand voice training with style guide + 5 examples
- [ ] Up to 200 generations per month capacity
- [ ] Plain text / Markdown output format
- [ ] Basic output validation (format + length checks)
- [ ] User guide PDF with setup and usage instructions
- [ ] Local setup on client's machine
- [ ] 1 round of revisions
- [ ] 7 days post-launch support
- [ ] Delivery within 5 days

### Not Included
- Multiple content types
- Multi-model routing or fallback
- Structured JSON/CSV output
- Platform integrations (Shopify, WordPress, etc.)
- Grammar or brand compliance checks
- Analytics dashboard
- Video tutorials
- Cloud hosting
- API endpoint or web dashboard

## Client Information Request Template

Copy and send this to the client immediately after order is placed:

```
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for your order! I'm excited to build your AI content generation system.

To design the system that best fits your workflow, I need to understand your setup:

--- Your Business ---
1. What does your business sell / promote?
2. Who is your target audience?
3. What is your brand voice? (e.g., professional & authoritative, friendly & casual, 
   witty & bold — share a few adjectives)

--- Content Needs ---
4. What type(s) of content do you need generated?
   [ ] Product descriptions / ecommerce listings
   [ ] Social media posts (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn)
   [ ] Advertising copy (Google Ads, Facebook/Instagram Ads)
   [ ] Blog articles / SEO content
   [ ] Email marketing copy
   [ ] Short-video scripts
   [ ] Other: ______
5. How many pieces of content do you need per month (rough estimate)?
6. What platforms does this content get published to?

--- Your Data ---
7. Do you have a product catalog? (CSV, Excel, or database export)
8. Do you have examples of content you love? (links or screenshots)
9. Do you have brand guidelines, style guides, or past content to train the AI?

--- Technical (if known) ---
10. Do you have a preference for AI model?
    [ ] OpenAI GPT-4o
    [ ] Anthropic Claude
    [ ] DeepSeek
    [ ] No preference — use your recommendation
11. Do you already have an API key for the above? (needed for Basic package)

Share whatever you have — partial information is fine. We'll refine together.

Best,
[Your Name]
```
