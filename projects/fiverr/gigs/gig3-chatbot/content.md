## Gig Description

**Deploy a production-ready AI chatbot that answers customer questions instantly — powered by your own documents, product data, and knowledge base. Built with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) for accurate, hallucination-free responses.**

Your website visitors have questions. Your support team is overwhelmed. Your knowledge base sits unread. An AI chatbot trained on your own content changes everything — 24/7 instant answers, zero wait time, consistent brand voice, and a support load reduction of 60-80%. I build custom chatbots for ecommerce stores, SaaS platforms, and online education businesses that actually understand your products and policies.

**The technology:** RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is the industry-standard architecture for reliable AI chatbots. Unlike basic chatbots that make up answers, a RAG chatbot first searches your knowledge base for relevant information, then uses a large language model (GPT-4o, Claude, or open-source) to generate a natural, accurate response grounded in your actual data. The result: your customers get correct answers, not AI hallucinations.

**My process — 2-3x faster through AI-assisted development:** (1) Knowledge base preparation — I ingest your PDFs, website pages, product docs, FAQs, and support tickets into a vector database (Pinecone, Weaviate, or PGVector), (2) RAG pipeline — I build the retrieval + generation workflow with smart chunking, re-ranking, and source citation, (3) Chat interface — a customizable, embeddable widget that matches your brand, with conversation history and human handoff, (4) Testing & calibration — we run real question scenarios, measure accuracy, and tune the system, (5) Deployment — live on your website with monitoring, analytics, and ongoing support.

Whether you need a customer support agent for your Shopify store, an onboarding assistant for your SaaS, or a course Q&A bot for your online academy — I deliver a chatbot that feels like a real team member, not a robotic FAQ reader.

## FAQ

**1. What types of documents can the chatbot learn from?**
PDFs, Word documents, website pages (via URL crawl), Google Docs, Notion pages, CSV product catalogs, JSON data, markdown files, and plain text. If your content lives in a database or a custom CMS, I can build a custom connector for it. Basically: if a human can read it, the chatbot can learn from it.

**2. How accurate is it? Will it give wrong answers?**
RAG chatbots are significantly more accurate than standard AI chatbots because every answer is grounded in your actual documents. For well-structured knowledge bases, accuracy rates of 90-95%+ are standard. The Standard and Premium packages include re-ranking and source citation, so you (and your customers) can always trace an answer back to its source. I also build guardrails that make the chatbot say "I don't know" instead of guessing when the answer is not in your knowledge base.

**3. Do I need to provide an OpenAI API key?**
For the Basic package, yes — you bring your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key (cost is typically $0.002-0.01 per conversation, very affordable even at scale). For Standard and Premium, I provide managed API access within the package price up to a generous monthly conversation limit.

**4. How does this compare to off-the-shelf chatbot platforms like Intercom or Tidio?**
Off-the-shelf platforms give you a generic chatbot that follows decision-tree logic. My solution gives you a truly intelligent agent that understands natural language and can answer any question covered by your knowledge base — no manual flowchart building required. And unlike SaaS products that charge $100-500/month indefinitely, you own the code and can self-host to eliminate recurring costs.

**5. Can the chatbot handle conversations in my native language?**
Yes. The underlying AI models natively support 50+ languages. I configure the system to detect the user's language and respond in the same language. For the Standard and Premium packages, I also validate translation quality and ensure your non-English knowledge base content is indexed correctly.
